Seeing scenes one photon at a time.

I am a Ph.D. scholar in image processing at IIT Madras. I work on photon-limited imaging, 3D scene reconstruction and neural rendering.

My research turns the binary frames of single-photon cameras into colour HDR images and into 3D scenes you can view from new angles.

5 publications Best IAPR Paper Award, CVIP 2024 Samsung R&D research intern

Research

Single-photon imaging

Image-to-image pipelines that convert binary SPC frames into colour HDR images, built with GANs (Pix2Pix, Pix2PixHD, CycleGAN) and diffusion bridges (Brownian Bridge, Schrödinger Bridge). Trained on datasets from a physics-based SPC simulator grounded in Poisson statistics.

3D from binary images

Novel view synthesis from SPC images using NeRF, Gaussian Splatting and generalizable NeRFs, and how single-frame denoising affects the 3D reconstruction that follows.

Scene relighting

A separate line of work from my internship at Samsung R&D Institute: total relighting of real-world scenes with deep generative models, using synthetic Blender datasets with controllable lighting.
